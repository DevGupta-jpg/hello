Things to Check Before Booking

✓

Included in Your Stay

Standard essentials covered for every resident.

  • Fully Furnished Room: Individual bed, mattress, pillow, personal almirah, study table, and chair.
  • High-Speed Wi-Fi: Fiber broadband access across rooms and common areas.
  • RO Drinking Water: 24×7 purified chilled and normal drinking water.
  • Daily Housekeeping: Regular room cleaning and twice-daily washroom cleaning.
  • 24×7 Water & Security: Continuous water supply, geyser hot water, CCTV, and managed entry.
i

Things to Confirm Before Booking

Transparent checklist for complete clarity.

  • Security Deposit & Refund Policy: Confirm the exact deposit amount and refund timeline with management.
  • Electricity Sub-meter Rates (AC Usage): Confirm the applicable AC electricity billing and unit rate.
  • Meal Plan / Tiffin Timings: Confirm the weekly food menu, breakfast/lunch/dinner timings, and meal package details.
  • Notice Period & Guest Policy: Confirm notice requirements, visitor entry timings, and gate rules before moving in.
  • Balcony / Non-Balcony Preference: Confirm the preferred room option and current availability before booking.
